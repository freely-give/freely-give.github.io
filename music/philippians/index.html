About​

The lyrics come from the Berean Standard Bible and the music was made with a paid account of Suno, which gives the song rights to the creator (Freely Give). All of the songs were then dedicated to the public domain. You can download them by pressing the three dots on the audio player and choosing "Download". Share or modify them as you like. No attribution required.

If you would like to help put more of the Bible to music and dedicate it to the public domain, please contact Freely Give.
